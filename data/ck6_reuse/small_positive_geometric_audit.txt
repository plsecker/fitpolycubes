STRUCTURAL NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------
  * CROSS-PIECE CONGRUENCE: I-V25-S3 == X-V25-S2 -- the same figure tiled by both pieces (5x3x3 cross-tower: 5 stacked X crosses = 5 parallel I-bars).
  * No cross-entry enantiomer pairs can exist: mirror images share one full-O_h canonical, hence one entry.
  * All 52 figures contain inversion (CK6 or higher), hence ALL are achiral: proper-rotation and O_h canonicalisations agree per figure; 51 distinct figures under proper rotations.
  * Most exact-CK6 figures have corpus_entries = 2: they are closed under TWO conjugate centre-type CK6 placements (the 19-T phenomenon); higher-symmetry figures have exactly 1.
  * The I-pentacube family spans bars (V=5, V=15 straight bars, V=25 1x1x25 bar and 1x5x5 slab), collinear stacks, and 8 exact-CK6 free-form I-bar figures at V=25.
