### Verification statement (task 2)

Every row above is verified as **published-solvable, not prime**:

* solution cell reads `1+` (>= 1 published solution), matching the
  interpretation codified in `catalogues/s_catalogue.py`
  ("Page `1+` entries: solvable boxes that are NOT prime (no prime
  marker)");
* the marker cell is empty — no `prime`/`prime minimal` claim is
  inferred or assumed;
* the printed piece count satisfies `volume == 5 * pieces`
  (77/77 pass).

Prime-marked rows are **excluded** from this package's promotion set;
they are used only for the RAW_PRIMES cross-check (section 4).

Of the 77 rows, **52** lie within the replay window
dims <= 60 and **25** outside (`3x23x{150..275}`, `3x24x300`,
`3x25x{63,74}`, `5x10x{61..65,67,68,71}`, `5x11x{65..95}`,
`4x12x75`, `4x13x75`). All 77 would enter `PUBLISHED_SOLUTIONS`; the
102-box replay covers the 52 in-window rows plus their derivations.
