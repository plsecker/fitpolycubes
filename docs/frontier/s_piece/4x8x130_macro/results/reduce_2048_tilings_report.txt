## Symmetry group used
- Reducer mode: 48 symmetries (6 axis permutations x 8 sign patterns,
  rotations + reflections, translation-normalized to origin).
- The fixed 4x8x130 box (distinct dimensions) has true symmetry group
  Z2^3 of order 8: midplane reflections x->3-x, y->7-y, z->129-z.
- The 48-group is a superset, but box-external transforms map a 4x8x130
  tiling to a tiling of a different box (e.g. 8x4x130), which cannot
  coincide with any other 4x8x130 tiling. Hence the classes induced on
  the 2048 tilings are exactly the 8-element box-symmetry orbits.
- Verified empirically: the 8-group orbit partition and the 48-group
  class partition on the 2048 tilings are identical (528 classes each).
