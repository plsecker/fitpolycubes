- Summary: Extensive 2D tiling and packing data for pentomino 5/47. The page covers 2D only, with many prime rectangles across widths from 12 to 30, credited to Postl (1998), Reid (1999), and Shirakawa (2015).

## Notes

- No explicit "Complete" statement is given.
- No 3D, 4D, or 5D data is present.
- The 2D minimal prime is 20x20 (23 solutions, credited to Postl 1998).
- Many widths are documented as impossible for certain lengths (e.g., widths 13, 15, 19 are entirely impossible).
- Credits span Postl (1998), Reid (1999), and Shirakawa (2015).
- The 30xN family is documented with individual lengths from 30 to 45.
