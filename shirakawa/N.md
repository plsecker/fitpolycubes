# N

- URL: https://puzzlewillbeplayed.com/Shirakawa/N.html
- Page title: Shirakawa's Box Packing Collection : N
- Last updated: Mar 22, 2015
- Summary: Tiling and packing data for the N-pentomino (5/8). The page states "3D Complete." It lists prime boxes across 3D and 4D, with all entries credited to Postl (1998) and one 4D entry to Shirakawa (2015).

## Notes

- "3D Complete." is stated at the top.
- No 2D data is present.
- The 3D minimal prime is 2x4x5 (2 solutions, credited to Postl 1998).
- All 3D entries are credited to Postl (1998).
- The 5x5x5 box has 4 solutions (prime).
- 4D includes only one entry: 3x3x4x5 (1+ solutions, prime, credited to Shirakawa 2015).
