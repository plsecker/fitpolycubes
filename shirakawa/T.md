# T

- URL: https://puzzlewillbeplayed.com/Shirakawa/T.html
- Page title: Shirakawa's Box Packing Collection : T
- Last updated: Feb 18, 2015
- Summary: The largest and most detailed page in the collection, covering the T-pentomino (5/7). It lists hundreds of box configurations across 3D, 4D, and 5D, with extensive data on primes, composites, and impossible boxes. The 3D section alone covers dimensions from 3x3xN through 10x10x10.

## Notes

- No explicit "Complete" statement is given.
- The 3D section is extremely long, covering thousands of line items. Many width-3 boxes are impossible; the page systematically documents which lengths work for each width.
- 3D minimal prime is 3x10x10 (1 solution, credited to Göbel 1989). Another minimal prime is 5x5x12 (1 solution, credited to Beeler 1990).
- Credits span Göbel (1989), Beeler (1990), Sillke (1993-2013), Postl (1998), van de Konijnenberg (2009), and Shirakawa (2013-2015).
- The 5x8xN family is documented with extraordinary granularity, listing individual lengths from 100 to 227+ with solution counts.
- 4D data includes 3x3x3x5 (impossible), 3x3x3x10 (54 solutions), and many others.
- 5D includes 3x3x3x3x5 and 3x3x3x4x5.
- The "s:0" notation appears for some families (e.g., 4x11xN, 4x13xN), likely indicating semigroup-based impossibility.
