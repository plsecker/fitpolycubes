# W

- URL: https://puzzlewillbeplayed.com/Shirakawa/W.html
- Page title: Shirakawa's Box Packing Collection : W
- Last updated: Feb 18, 2015
- Summary: Tiling and packing data for the W-pentomino (5/10). The page states "3D Complete." It lists extensive prime box data across 3D and 4D, with credits spanning Sillke (1994), Postl (1998), and Shirakawa (2014-2015).

## Sections

- Pentomino (5/10)
  - 3D
  - 4D

## References

- [Tiling and Packing results of Torsten Sillke](http://www.mathematik.uni-bielefeld.de/~sillke/results.html)
- [Michael Reid's box collection](http://www.cflmath.com/Polyomino/boxes.html)

## Notes

- "3D Complete." is stated at the top.
- No 2D data is present.
- The 3D minimal prime is 5x6x6 (2 solutions, credited to Sillke 1994).
- Many 3D boxes are explicitly listed as impossible, including 3x[3-6]xN, 3x7x10, 3x7x15, and 4x[4-9]xN.
- The 4x5xN family is documented extensively with individual lengths from 19 to 46.
- 4D data is credited to Shirakawa (2015) and includes several impossible configurations and a few primes.
- The "s:0" notation appears for some 4D families.
