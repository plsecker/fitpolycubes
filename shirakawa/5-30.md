## Notes

- No 2D or 3D data is present.
- 4D states "2xXxYxZ" and "3x3xYxZ" are all 0 (impossible), credited to Shirakawa 2015.
- 5D minimal prime is 2x2x2x3x5 (1+ solutions, credited to Shirakawa 2015).
- 6D includes a single entry: 2x2x2x2x2x5 (1+ solutions, prime, credited to Shirakawa 2015).
