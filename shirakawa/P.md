## Notes

- Very sparse data. Only the 2x5 minimal prime is listed for 2D 1-sided and 2D.
- 2D free includes 7x15 (33,631 solutions, prime, credited to Klarner 1969).
- 3D only lists 3x3x5 (153 solutions, prime, credited to Sillke).
- No 4D data is present.
