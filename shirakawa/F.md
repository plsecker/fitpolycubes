# F

- URL: https://puzzlewillbeplayed.com/Shirakawa/F.html
- Page title: Shirakawa's Box Packing Collection : F
- Last updated: Mar 20, 2015
- Summary: Tiling and packing data for the F-pentomino (5/9). The page states "3D Complete." It covers 3D and 4D with extensive prime box data, primarily credited to Sillke (1993-1996) and Shirakawa (2015).

## Notes

- "3D Complete." is stated at the top.
- No 2D data is present.
- The 3D section documents that boxes 3x[3-5]xN are impossible. The minimal prime is 3x6x10 (62 solutions, credited to Hamlyn).
- Many boxes are listed with explicit 0 solutions (impossible), systematically documenting which configurations fail.
- 4D data is credited entirely to Shirakawa (2015), including 3x3x4x5 as the 4D minimal prime.
- A correction note is appended: "Sillke overlooked 4x6x[20,25] and 5x6x10."
