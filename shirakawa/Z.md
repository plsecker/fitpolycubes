# Z

- URL: https://puzzlewillbeplayed.com/Shirakawa/Z.html
- Page title: Shirakawa's Box Packing Collection : Z
- Last updated: Feb 18, 2015
- Summary: Extensive tiling and packing data for the Z-pentomino (5/11). The page is very long, covering 3D, 4D, and 5D boxes. The 3D section systematically documents impossibility for small widths and lists primes for larger configurations, primarily credited to Shirakawa (2013-2015).

## Notes

- No explicit "Complete" statement is given.
- The 3D section documents extensive impossibility results: 3x[3-22]xN are all impossible, with the first solutions appearing at 3x23x150.
- The 3D minimal prime is 6x10x10 (11+ solutions, credited to Shindo 1997).
- The "s:0" notation appears for many families, indicating semigroup-based impossibility.
- 4D data is extensive, with many entries credited to Shirakawa (2013-2014). The 4D minimal prime is 3x4x5x5 (1+ solutions).
- 5D includes a single entry: 3x3x3x5x5 (credited to Shirakawa 2014).
- The 5x10xN family is documented with extraordinary granularity, listing individual lengths from 33 to 71.
