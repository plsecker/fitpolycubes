# U

- URL: https://puzzlewillbeplayed.com/Shirakawa/U.html
- Page title: Shirakawa's Box Packing Collection : U
- Last updated: Apr 14, 2015
- Summary: Tiling and packing data for the U-pentomino (5/5). The page states "Complete." for the 3D classification. It lists prime boxes across 3D and 4D, all credited to Sillke (1993).

## Notes

- "Complete." is stated at the top.
- No 2D data is present on this page.
- 3D minimal prime is 2x3x5 (1 solution). All 3D boxes are credited to Sillke (1993).
- 4D data includes three boxes: 2x2x4x5, 2x5x5x5, and 3x3x3x5, all credited to Sillke (1993).
- Several entries use the "1+" notation indicating more solutions exist than shown.
