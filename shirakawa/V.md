# V

- URL: https://puzzlewillbeplayed.com/Shirakawa/V.html
- Page title: Shirakawa's Box Packing Collection : V
- Last updated: Feb 18, 2015
- Summary: Extensive tiling and packing data for the V-pentomino (5/6). The page states "3D Complete. 4D Complete except for 4x5x5x5 and 5x5x5x5." It covers 3D, 4D, and 5D boxes, with many entries showing 0 solutions (impossible) and others listing primes.

## Notes

- "3D Complete. 4D Complete except for 4x5x5x5 and 5x5x5x5." is stated at the top.
- Many 3D boxes are explicitly listed as impossible (0 solutions), including 3x4x5, 3x5x10, 3x7x10, 4x4x5, 4x5x5, and 5x5x5.
- The 3D prime 3x5x6 (3 solutions) is the minimal prime.
- 4D data is extensive, with entries from 1993 (Sillke) and 2014 (Shirakawa).
- 5D includes a single entry: 3x3x3x3x5 (credited to Shirakawa 2014).
- A notable correction is appended: "Sillke says 5x5x12 is prime, but it is not prime, and 3x3x3x15 is impossible, but it is possible."
